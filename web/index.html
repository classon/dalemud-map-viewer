      About & credits

      An unofficial map viewer for the world of DaleMUD, the MUD that ran at Shadowdale. Rooms, mobs and items are read from the game's own world files, as published in the DaleMUD open source release. All of the world's text belongs to the people below.

      DaleMUD / ShadowMUD at Shadowdale

      M. Wilson (Celestian), D. Julitov (Aarcerak) and M. Antich (Hiisi).

      Areas were written by many builders, several of whom are named in the zone list or in the saved zone files, including Celestian, Stic, Alun, Sarge, Sarc, Kazgoroth, Euphrates, Tyr, Naril, Mason, Krell and Dalamar.

      SillyMUD

      Developed on machines at the University of Florida by J. Brothers, J. Sievert, K. McClelland, S. Gardner, R. Forsman and P. Martin. SillyMUD was based on DikuMUD.

      DikuMUD

      Original game idea, concept and design by Katja Nyboe (Superwoman), Tom Madsen (Stormbringer), Hans Henrik Stærfeldt (God), Michael Seifert (Papi) and Sebastian Hammer (Quinn). Developed at DIKU, the Department of Computer Science at the University of Copenhagen.
